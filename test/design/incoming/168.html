Answers to the four remaining questions
Which sort rests
Recently touched carries the check — it is the order :shelf defines, so the screen’s real promise is recency. 10’s caption is corrected from “most finishable” to match.
Does the hero follow
Yes — a filter that removes the hero promotes the next filtered row. The hero is the first ready row rather than a separate query, so a pinned unfiltered hero would let it disagree with the list under it.
Do filters persist
No. Sort persists between visits, filters last a session. A sort that survives reorders; a filter that survives hides — and a silently empty screen on launch is the worse failure. So the stale-filter band is not a real state and is not drawn.
Does Reset clear sort
No. It clears the filters only. Sort is the persisted thing, and one control doing two jobs is how a reset comes to mean two different things.
