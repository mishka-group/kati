A tick commits immediately — 94’s precedent, and membership is a thing you toggle rather than compose. So the failure has no Save to hang on: the tick goes back off with the reason in words, and the sheet stays open. Ticks arrive populated, like 73’s tracks — a sheet that opens blank invites the duplicate the tick exists to prevent. The third state for many titles is a dash: a half-fill reads as a progress bar and a count needs a column. No search field until a list count needs one — seven fit, and the number to watch is twelve.
