§3 — THE ANSWER, THEN THE COLUMN, THEN THE CARD
call_split
A prep belongs to a slot, not to a recipe. Oats soaked on Sunday night are prepped for Monday breakfast — not for oats. Prep Sunday and Wednesday from the same recipe and those are two preps; the recipe has not changed.
The three answers, and why two lose
block
Per recipe
prepped_at on Recipe
One column, and wrong. Prepping Sunday’s oats would mark Wednesday’s done, and a second prep has nowhere to go.
block
Per day
a prepped list on the day
Right about time, wrong about what. Two meals prepped on one evening cannot be told apart.
check
Per slot
prepped_at on MealPlanSlot
A slot is already the pairing of a meal with a date. The prep has a date because the slot does, and it needs no reset rule.
info
No reset rule is needed, which is how you can tell the answer is right. A slot passes when its day passes; a per-recipe flag would need a nightly job to clear it, and a job that clears state at midnight is a bug with a schedule.
