Kati records and shows every amount in one currency, chosen once and never converted. Changing it changes the symbol and the formatting — it does not touch a single stored figure.
Why not convert
Kati has no server, so it cannot know what yesterday’s rate was — and a converted history that quietly used today’s rate would be worse than no conversion at all.
